About AI Data Lab

Through interdisciplinary and data-intensive research, the lab collaborates with faculty and students while supporting digital humanities through workshops, shared resources, and research infrastructure.

Lab Director

Dr. Seul Lee

Dr. Seul Lee is an Assistant Professor in the School of Information at Florida State University and the founder of the AI Data Lab, where she leads interdisciplinary research on how artificial intelligence shapes the information people create, encounter, trust, and use.
