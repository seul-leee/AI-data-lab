The AI Data Lab · Florida State University

AI, Literacy,
Ethics, and Society.

Led by Dr. Seul Lee, the AI Data Lab examines how artificial intelligence reshapes the creation, interpretation, and use of information. Drawing on mixed methods, we investigate whose knowledge informs these systems, whose values they reflect, and how people learn to engage with them critically and responsibly.
